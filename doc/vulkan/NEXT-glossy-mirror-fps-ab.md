## 1. The task after that: fps cost of the traced glossy mirror

`glossyrt` (live switch in `<work>\rt-debug.txt`, default 0) traces rays for glossy surfaces (gloss 0.9 and up) of the lighting pass; the mirror in the Larsson room is the test. 0 = the stock hero probe mirror ("legacy mirror"), 1 = traced. Everything Adam has measured so far (about 120 fps in front of the mirror with ray shadows on) was `glossyrt=0`. The traced mirror cost is not measured cleanly: the handoff estimates +0.3 to 0.4 ms GPU per frame with the mirror at about 15 % of the screen. Needed: the steady-state fps difference, and which of the two paths to make the default.

**Baseline (2026-10-13, lean config, `glossyrt=0`, 19 logins, readings every 10 s):** settled median 122 fps, mean 119 (first reading while the world still loads: 99). One login that failed to start read 38 and 62: drop it. Config of that baseline: `LL_VK_RT=1`, `LL_VK_RT_SHADOWS=1`, `LL_VK_RT_MATERIALS=1`, `LL_VK_PRESENT=immediate`, no `LL_VK_PHASES`, no draw stamps, 2558x1360 window, own avatar standing in the mirror room circle.

**How to run it (already set up):**

```
powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File <work>\hunt.ps1 -Name glossy-ab1 -Runs 6 -Materials -Lean -Settle 60 -Hold 45 -Configs "glossyrt=0|glossyrt=1|glossyrt=0|glossyrt=1|glossyrt=0|glossyrt=1"
python <work>\ab-fps-report.py glossy-ab1 --skip 1
```

- `hunt.ps1 -Lean` = no phase timing, no draw stamps, no opstats (so the GPU runs at its normal load: the diagnostic config leaves it at about 60 %). `-Configs` switches the live keys of `rt-debug.txt` between phases of ONE login, so the sun, the sim and the avatar are identical on both sides (comparing separate logins drifts). `-Hold` is the phase length in seconds, `-Settle` the wait after login before phase 1.
- The viewer's `display_stats : FPS` line comes every 10 s and is the average of the 10 s before it; `ab-fps-report.py` takes the readings that lie wholly inside a phase and prints per-phase means, per-setting means and the mean difference of each change. `--skip 1` drops the first reading of each phase (the first switch to 1 builds the glossy shader permutation, it hitches).
- `hunt.ps1` stops at the first non-zero exit or "device lost": a login error also stops it ("CRASH at run N" without "device lost" in the log is a login problem: free the account with `<work>\free-account.ps1`, it logs in through Radegast; the hunt does that itself when the last log shows no "Goodbye!").
- Adam wants steady-state numbers in the mirror room, ideally also standing so that the mirror fills more of the screen (a second set of runs with Adam in place; he can stand there with the toggle script instead: `<work>\run-viewer-clean-rtcheck-toggle.bat` + `rt-toggle-check.ps1` flip other keys the same way; write a copy for `glossyrt` if needed). GPU ms per pass (not just fps) needs the diagnostic config (`LL_VK_PHASES=1`, drop `-Lean`): the stats line "GPU time by render pass" then names the glossy lighting cost; it changes timing, so use it for the ms figure only, not for the fps.
- **Do not touch the viewer window during a session** (and do not give another window the focus): an unfocused viewer drops to single-digit fps for the readings it covers (a 9 fps reading in the smoke test was Adam typing elsewhere). `ab-fps-report.py` drops readings under `--min-fps` (default 30) for that reason, but the phase then has fewer readings.
- Read the **per-setting medians** from the report, not the means: a reading that overlaps a focus loss is only partly depressed (77 and 69 in the smoke test) and survives the 30 fps floor. Smoke test (one login, 3 phases per setting): median `glossyrt=0` 105.8, `glossyrt=1` 103.1, about 2.7 fps (2.5 %) behind, not conclusive yet.
- Do at least 6 logins (36 phase pairs is plenty), look at the spread between logins before trusting a 1 to 2 fps difference.

**Then decide:** default of `glossyrt`, the gloss cutoff (0.9), legacy shiny surfaces (not traced), and the probe views still in `softenLightF.glsl` (can go once the traced path is the default). Also the item "DLSS ray reconstruction guide data" is next on the plan after glossy.

## 1b. Two things Adam saw in the traced mirror (2026-10-13, after the crash fix)

- **Avatar flicker with `glossyrt=1`** (only with the traced mirror, not the legacy one): a fast flicker that makes the avatar in the mirror look half transparent. The own avatar is never left out of the ray scene (`avlog=1` in `rt-debug.txt` writes `<work>
t-avatars.log`; no SKIP lines in 90 s), its pose is 0 to 2 frames old on about a third of the calls. What is regular: two frames of three have 3 scene calls and one has only call 0 (9924 : 4962 frames in the log), a 3-frame cycle (about 33 Hz): the extra calls are the hero probe / reflection probe faces (the comment at the call counter in `llvkrt.cpp`: "a second scene call in a frame (a reflection probe, a snapshot) has its own camera"). All calls of a frame share one TLAS and one geometry table per frame slot, so the lighting pass of the main view may trace a scene that a probe call rebuilt with its own camera and culling. Test in progress: the live switch `single=1` (only the first scene call of a frame builds) against `single=0` with `glossyrt=1`: `hunt.ps1 -Name x -Runs 1 -Materials -Lean -Settle 40 -Hold 50 -Configs "glossyrt=1;single=0|glossyrt=1;single=1|..."` while Adam watches **Result: `single=1` made no visible difference (Adam), so the shared TLAS is not the cause.** Next experiments, cheapest first: (1) DLSS / DLAA off (a traced reflection of a moving avatar has no motion vectors of its own; the upscaler may reject its history every frame: a fast flicker that looks semi-transparent), needs a restart with the setting changed; (2) still camera and still avatar: if it stops, it is temporal (DLSS, pose age); (3) `rigged=0` (live): the avatar leaves the ray scene; (4) the per-pixel noise of the glossy rays (`rt_noise_frame`) with one ray per pixel and no accumulation: a thin avatar edge flickers between hit and miss.
- **The avatar flicker depends on the sun (found from the sun log, 2026-10-13):** seen at sun elevation 20 to 1 degrees (smoke test, `glossy-flicker`), gone in every session after sunset (elevation -22 to -32, the moon). Steady `glossyrt=1` at night, and toggling `glossyrt` 0/1 at night: no flicker. So test it with a locked daytime environment (sun 10 to 20 degrees up; Adam can lock the environment). Hypothesis (untested): the sun shadow ray of the hit shading (`rtSunRay` from `rtReflection`) starts on the avatar's own structure, which is refitted every frame with a pose 0 to 2 frames old (3-frame cycle, the pace of the flicker), so it hits its own older geometry on some frames and not on others. Experiments in daylight: `rigged=0` (avatar out of the ray scene), a larger shadow ray offset / `tmin` for avatar hits, no shadow ray for avatar hits (shade them lit) and see whether the flicker follows. `single=1` is NOT usable (the traced mirror then misses and shows the legacy mirror, the room lighting flickers: the main view traces a probe call's scene).
- **State of the flicker hunt at the end of 2026-10-13 (what is known, what is excluded).** Reproduces with the locked Afternoon sky (sun about 60 degrees up) and `glossyrt=1`; not with `glossyrt=0`, not at night. Burst captures (`vk-burst.ps1` while `glossyrt=1`, difference of the frames, in `<work>\strobe-glossy`) show two alternating states: in one the reflected avatar is complete, in the other its BODY (torso, skirt, legs: the opaque skinned meshes) is missing and the floor of the room shows there, while hair, hand and socks stay. The rasterised avatar is fine. Hit/miss view (`glossyrt=3`, burst in `strobe-hitmiss`): the whole mirror area is steadily green, so rays hit something in both states (the floor, in the missing-body state); only the wall signs flip red now and then. The own avatar is never skipped from the scene (`avlog=1`: no SKIP lines), always 44 draws in the structure at the sampled frames. Scene calls per frame (SCENECALL lines in `rt-avatars.log`): the main view is always the LAST call of its frame, preceded by 0 to 2 hero-probe faces (camera at the probe, six cube directions) or the reflection probe (64 m up). **Excluded by live phases (30 s each, beeps marking the switch, Adam watching):** `skinperiod=1`, `skinfast=0`, `rigged=0` (avatar gone, window then steady), `single=1` (breaks the traced mirror and flickers the room lighting), `dynamic=1/2`, `delta=0`, `tableverify=1` (no mismatch logged), `mats=0` (device lost when switched live: now guarded, `glossyrt` reads 0 when `mats=0`), `check=0 skipbad=0`, `serial=1`, `norefit=1`, `skinreach=5000` and `=3`, `LL_VK_FRAMES=6`, no sun shadow ray on hits (`glossyrt=20`), shadow ray offset 0.15 m (`glossyrt=21`), 1024 instead of 64 masked candidates per reflection ray (`glossyrt=22`). Also worth trying: capture bursts with `glossyrt=1` and `avlog=1` at once and match the avatar's per-call rows (`processed`, `refit`, `data_age`, the new SCENECALL lines) to the two states by wall time.
- **UPDATE, end of 2026-10-13: the avatar dropout is FIXED, the far objects and the camera-move flicker are not.** Cause of the avatar dropout (found by matching timestamped burst captures to `rt-avatars.log`): the own avatar's structure was built only from the draws named in THIS frame (`d.mNotedTag != av_tag -> continue` in the avatar loop of `buildScene`), and not every pass that names the avatar's opaque body and clothing runs every frame, so the structure alternated between 28 and 44 geometries and the body vanished from the traced reflection on the 28 frames (the log's last column `built_draws` showed it). Fix: draws named within the last `keep` frames (8) stay in the structure over their last skinned output (`mOut`), only draws named now are skinned again, and stale draws must still have the same buffers (epoch, address, size) they were named with (otherwise: spikes in the shadow while an avatar loads, Adam's report). Live A/B switch `avatarkeep=1` restores the old behaviour. Adam: phases with the fix = avatar steady, old = dropout. **Still open:** (1) far objects in the reflection (globe and stand, window frame and glass, wall pictures) disappear on some frames, in step with the old avatar dropout frames and also with the fix (the burst difference image and crops are in `<work>\strobe-corr`: `diff-outside.png`, `far-sheet.png`: in the bad state they are replaced by plain brick wall), (2) Adam still sees some flicker when the camera moves with the fix. The scene call pattern per viewer frame: two hero-probe face calls (camera 14,61,80) then the main call (14,54,80); about every fourth frame also one reflection-probe call (14,54,144); some frames lack one of the three. Next: log the instance count of every scene call (the `SCENEINST` line in `buildScene` is inside `if (diag_on())`: move it out, it is only written with `LL_VK_DIAG=1` now) and see whether the main call's instance count dips on the frames where the far objects vanish; if so find which groups are named by which passes (`sGroups`, the keep window of cached passes, `keep=8`), the same exact-frame-or-keep logic may drop whole groups; also test whether the camera-move flicker is the same thing or only one-ray-per-pixel aliasing (still-camera burst vs orbit burst: `<work>\strobe-still`, `strobe-orbit`).
- **The traced mirror looks like the diffuse buffer (RenderBufferVisualization 0), not like the lit scene**: not a setting. `rtReflection` (`shadowUtil.glsl`) shades a hit as `(ambient + sun * N.L * shadow) * albedo + emissive`: diffuse only, no specular, no normal map, no roughness, no point lights, no probe ambient. The quality gap to the stock mirror is this shading. Improve the hit shading (the material table already has normal and metallic-roughness textures and factors) before choosing the default.

## 3. Switches and tools worth knowing (all in the commits of 2026-10-13)

| What | How |
|---|---|
| Lean verdict runs | `hunt.ps1 -Lean` (RT_NO_PHASES=1, draw stamps 0) |
| Ray scene range check (on by default) | `check=` / `skipbad=` in `<work>\rt-debug.txt`, `LL_VK_RT_CHECK`; cost line "range check CPU" in the `rt scene` stats |
| Crash diagnosis if a device lost returns | `RT_DRAW_STAMPS=2` (hunt does it without `-Lean`) + `LL_VK_DRAW_SERIAL=1` (exact faulting draw); `LL_VK_ADDR_LOG=1` (what held the fault page); use them one at a time, together they hid the crash once |
| Old behaviours for A/B | `LL_VK_RING_STALE=1` (the crash bug comes back within a few logins) |
| Validation | `RT_VALIDATE=1` (+ `RT_GPUAV=1` for GPU-assisted: slow, `-Settle 70`); `rtcheck`: `hunt.ps1 -Name x -Runs 2 -Materials` with those env vars set in the same PowerShell call |
| Flushes | VkPhases stats line "mid-frame flushes (each waits for the GPU)" by cause; first 60 also logged one by one |
| Sun | log line `sun direction ... elevation, azimuth, sky '<name>'` (first frame and whenever it moves half a degree) |
