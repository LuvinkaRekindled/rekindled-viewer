## 4. Traps found on the way here
- The scene table is one persistent buffer read by frames in flight: never write per-frame data (palettes, anything) into its rows from the host; copy rows into scratch for the frame (`mRTable`, `alpha_rows`).
- Resident tables are write-combined memory: no CPU reads of them (the CPU keeps its own copies).
- A pull program that cannot draw a refused draw logs once per program and reason in `LLVertexBuffer::pullDraw`; look there first when something goes missing after adding a class.
- Hero / mirror probes cost about 2 ms a frame when a mirror is in view (extra scene render and cascades); a worn mirror of another avatar is ignored by `FSHeroProbeIgnoreOthersAttachments`; a mirror prim in view explains a sudden slowdown with everything else unchanged.
