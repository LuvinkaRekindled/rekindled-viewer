About Rekindled

Rekindled is Firestorm with a new engine under the hood. We rebuilt the renderer on Vulkan and spread the work across all those CPU cores you paid for. Everything you know from Firestorm is still here.

No OpenGL

The renderer talks to your graphics card through Vulkan and nothing else. OpenGL is gone, code and all.

  • Bindless textures, plus culling and sorting on the GPU
  • Texture animation and particles run on the GPU too
  • Shaders and pipelines get cached, so start-up stays quick

Built for many cores

In a busy region, one thread was holding everything up. So we moved the work off it.

  • A render thread that does the recording for the GPU
  • A shared pool of workers for visibility, shadows, avatar animation and textures
  • OpenAL audio with its own audio thread

Ray traced shadows

If your card has ray tracing hardware, the sun's shadows can be ray traced and cleaned up right on the GPU. It's a setting, so you can see what it costs you and decide.

Upscaling

Draws the picture smaller and scales it back up, with cleaner edges than the usual anti-aliasing. Supported on modern AMD and NVIDIA dedicated GPUs.

Tested on

AMD and Intel processors, on Windows and Linux, with a GeForce RTX 3060 (8 GB) and an RTX 5060 Ti (16 GB). Your results depend on your region and your machine.

Recommended

  • Turn on Preferences > Graphics > Hardware Settings > Compression to save VRAM.
  • Try Preferences > Graphics > Vulkan > Upscaling at 67%. Hit OK to save it, then restart the viewer.
  • If you're getting more FPS than you need, try turning on Vsync to keep your GPU and CPU cooler in Preferences > Graphics > Rendering.
